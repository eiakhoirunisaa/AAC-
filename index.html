User Guide 💡

👤 Multiple Profiles

Use the profile dropdown at the top to switch between different users on the same device. Each profile has its own separate word boards, categories, and voice recordings. Tap Manage Profiles to add, rename, or delete profiles.

✕ Remove a Word from the Sentence

Tap the red ✕ button on the top-right of any word in the sentence bar to remove it. You can also tap anywhere on the word card to remove it.

➕ Add Routine

Tap Edit at the bottom to enter edit mode. A blue ➕ Add Routine button appears at the end of the tab row. Tap it to add a new routine category. Use the ✏️ and ✕ badges on any tab to edit or delete it.

🎨 Auto Grouping by Part of Speech

Words are automatically sorted by part of speech as listed below.

🟨 Pronoun / Kata Ganti Nama
🟩 Verb / Kata Kerja
🟦 Adjective / Kata Adjektif
🩷 Adverb / Kata Adverba
🍑 Prep & Conj / Kata Sendi
🌟 Question Word / Kata Tanya
💠 Interjection / Kata Seru
🟧 Noun / Kata Nama

🎙️ Custom Voice — Record or Upload

For each word, you can either record your own voice (with noise suppression and automatic leading-silence trimming) or upload an existing audio file (.mp3, .wav, .ogg, .m4a, .webm). Once a custom voice exists, it replaces the default TTS during playback.
