🎨 Color Code System

Tiles use soft pastel background colors for easy category recognition:

🟧 Orange: Noun
🟩 Green: Verb
🟦 Blue: Adjective
🟨 Yellow: Social
🟪 Purple: Routine

📚 All Words Category

The All Words tab shows every word from all your categories in one clean, uniform grid. No badges, no extra gaps — just all your words ready to tap.

Features & Customization

• Tap tiles to add to the sentence strip.
• Tap Edit to edit/delete words and categories.
• Add custom images or emojis to suit your routine.
• Tiles have a colorful 3D effect — they lift on hover and press down when tapped.
