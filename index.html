User Guide 💡

🎨 Auto Grouping by Part of Speech

Words are automatically sorted by part of speech — Nouns first, then Verbs, Adjectives, Social, then Routine. Groups flow seamlessly across rows with no gaps. The tile color tells you the group:

🟧 Orange: Noun
🟩 Green: Verb
🟦 Blue: Adjective
🟨 Yellow: Social
🟪 Purple: Routine

🖼️ Local Image Assets

All word tiles load images from the assets/ folder. Transparent PNGs blend seamlessly with the tile's pastel color.

❓ Question Button & Language Toggle

Tap the small ❓ button next to a category name to show its question list. Use the EN / BM toggle in the question header to switch languages.

🎙️ Custom Voice

Record your own voice for any tile. When you play a sentence, the app will use your recorded voice for those tiles automatically.
